ABOUT

Nokia Snake — a full-stack tribute to the Nokia 3310 classic.

  • 4 modes: Classic, Wrap, Endless, Maze
  • 3 difficulties, 3 food types, 6 power-ups
  • Obstacles unlock past 100 points
  • Accounts, leaderboards, achievements & stats
  • Flask + MongoDB backend, vanilla JS frontend

Secret: try the classic Konami code on your keyboard.
